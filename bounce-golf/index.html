Tap to aim, then hold HIT and release. Each hole starts with a random ball and its own ground physics. Sticky balls cling to any solid platform or screen wall, but not the bottom hazard. The final island has a longer green and sometimes a connected approach. Landing on the green turns your ball white without stopping its roll; once it settles, use a gentle putt towards the cup. Each level starts at 45° towards the hole and power starts at zero. Putting power is stronger: hold HIT longer for distant cups. If the ball falls into the themed hazard below the islands it is lost and that stroke still counts.
